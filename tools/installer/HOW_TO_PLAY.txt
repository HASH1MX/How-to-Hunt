How to Hunt

Playing alone: start How to Hunt, press Play and pick (or create) a world.

Playing with friends (over the internet, through Steam):
1. Start Steam and log in. The game connects players through Steam, where it shows as "Spacewar".
2. Start How to Hunt and press Multiplayer.
   - To host: press "Host on Steam", then press Esc in the game and "Copy join code". Send the code to your friends.
   - To join: paste the code and press Join (or pick a game from "Open games").
Everyone needs the same version of the game.

Your saved worlds and settings are stored in:
  %APPDATA%\Godot\app_userdata\How to Hunt
They are kept when you uninstall or update the game.
